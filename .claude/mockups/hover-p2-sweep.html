Hover effects that promise a click that isn't there

Four spots where the hover effect says something false: a card that lights up but doesn't open, two hover styles on one screen, a pencil phones never see, and a card that lifts even when you're pointing at its delete button.

The panes are live. Hover them on desktop. Phone frames act like a touch screen, with no hover.

What you are deciding

1. SEO and Moment cards: they stop lighting up on hover, because the card itself does nothing. Their buttons keep their own hover.

2. Your Week tiles: every tile you can click reacts the same way, lifting with a teal edge. Today the "Draft" tile turns grey instead.

3. Patient types pencil: it uses the shared reveal from #2832, so it always shows on phones and on keyboard focus.

4. Staff profile interview card: the card stops lifting. The title and arrow turn teal when you point at the part that opens; the delete button keeps its red hover.
