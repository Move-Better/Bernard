Why a human is in this loop. Captions reposition by formula, but the crop can't: trimming 285px off the top moves the subject from 26% to 16% of frame height while the caption margin stays a fixed 14% — so they converge by construction on any clip with the subject up high. Bernard proposes the nudge; the producer confirms. Every confirm-vs-drag is override signal for the confidence loop, so this step should get quieter over time.
