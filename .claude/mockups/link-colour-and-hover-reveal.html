Teal rows that look like links, and buttons phones never see

Two small problems with one cause: the app's base stylesheet paints every link teal, and several rows use hover to hide their buttons. Each section shows today and the change at desktop and phone width.

The panes are live. Hover the desktop rows, or press Tab to move through them. Phone frames act like a touch screen: there is no hover.

What you are deciding

1. Recap rows: story titles and names go back to dark text, and turn teal only when you point at them.

2. Hidden buttons: on phones they are always shown (in grey). On desktop they stay hidden until you point at the row, as today, but now also appear when you Tab to the row with the keyboard.

3. Scope: the audit named 5 places. I found 3 more with the same pattern (Book "exclude", and two "remove media" buttons in editors). I propose fixing all 8 with one shared style.
