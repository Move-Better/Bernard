Who is in this photo

Three places change. The Library gets a "Needs people" view, MediaDetail separates "Pictured" from "Uploaded by", and the uploader asks who is in the photo as a multi-select. The attributed-clinician field that videos use for voice and captions is untouched.

1. Library: Needs people

A new filter beside the existing ones. It lists photos with nobody tagged, so the 73 blank Move Better photos are a worklist. Selecting several tiles and tapping a name tags all of them at once. Names offered are current staff only.

Today one "Attributed to" dropdown does both jobs. On a photo it becomes "Who is in this photo" with several names allowed. "Uploaded by" is shown read-only from who actually uploaded it. Videos keep "Attributed to" and "Who's speaking" exactly as now.
