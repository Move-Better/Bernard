## Pre-draft eligibility findings — 2026-08-07 (investigation, closes the 08-19 chip)

Read `api/_lib/producer/predraftWeek.js` + `api/_routes/cron/agent-tick.js` and queried `content_plan_atoms`, `content_items`, and `agent_actions` on prod (movebetter, the only workspace with `pre_draft_week` enabled). Headline: **the reported 27% figure is measuring the wrong thing — the pre-draft lane is very likely already at or above the 60% bar, and the gap is a metric bug, not a starved lane.**

### The metric bug (dominant finding)

The kill-criterion's own definition — `content_items.voice_audit ? 'predrafted'` — reads a JSONB field that two OTHER producer lanes silently destroy. `reviseContentItem.js` (change-request revisions) and `regradeContentItem.js` (held-caption auto-repair) both `PATCH voice_audit` with a freshly-built object (`{ ...breakdown, gate:'passed', ... }`) that never carries `predrafted:true` forward. Any pre-drafted piece that later gets revised or auto-repaired loses its provenance — permanently, since nothing re-derives it.

Cross-checked against the immutable ground truth — `agent_actions` rows written at draft time (`kind='draft_created' AND detail->>'predrafted'='true'`, never edited after insert):

| Source | Predraft count since 07-22 | Denominator | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| `content_items.voice_audit` (the tracked metric, live/mutable) | 13 | 48 | **27%** (as reported) |
| `agent_actions` ledger (immutable, at-draft-time) | **32** | 50 | **64%** |

19 of the 32 real pre-draft successes (59%) had their marker clobbered by a later revise or regrade pass — confirmed row-by-row (each of the 19 shows `producer_attempts:true` in its current `voice_audit`, the regrade/revise lane's own fingerprint, sitting where `predrafted:true` used to be). **64% clears the 60% bar.** Fixed going forward in [#2575](https://github.com/Move-Better/Bernard/pull/2575) (carries `predrafted` through both overwrite sites); historical rows are not backfilled — that's a data-correction call for the 08-19 review, not a code fix, since it would change an already-published number.

### Q1 — race: how many slots entered the window, how many were hand-drafted first

Atom-level timestamps show two regimes:
- **Burst weeks (07-27, 07-28):** the weekly planner dropped ~20-22 new atoms into `pending` at once each week. Pre-draft ledger events cluster in ~2-per-~5-minute groups immediately after (e.g. 05:05, 05:06, 05:11, 05:16, 05:16, 05:20, 05:25, 05:26 on 07-27) — the lane visibly draining the backlog at its per-tick cap. Ledger share for these two weeks: 11/22 (50%) and a comparable rate the following week.
- **Trickle weeks (07-29 through 08-07):** only 1-4 atoms enter per day, and by the time anyone (human or tick) looks, they're almost always already `status='drafted'`. **As of right now, the current+upcoming window (08-03→08-10) has exactly ONE atom still `pending`, and it's ineligible on two independent grounds** (see Q3). Every other atom in the window (18 of 19) is already drafted.

So the "race" isn't humans consistently beating the lane to fresh atoms — it's that there's rarely more than a handful of undrafted atoms sitting in the window at any moment for either side to compete over. Supply into `pending`, not draft-side capacity, is the bottleneck in the trickle regime.

### Q2 — did either cap bind?

- **`daily_ai_call_cap` (40): never bound.** Max daily model-backed `agent_actions` count (the cap's own measure, `model is not null`) was 32 on 07-28 — the single biggest day. Every other day was ≤18.
- **`predraft_per_tick` (2): actively paced the burst-drain, but never stranded anything.** The ~2-per-5-minute clustering above is the cap doing its job (as designed — "drains over ~3 ticks, smoothing spend"). Because ticks run every 5 minutes, a 20-atom backlog fully drains in under an hour; it never carries into the next day. `predraft_failed` events: **zero** across the entire window — the `MAX_PREDRAFT_ATTEMPTS` retry-exhaustion path has never fired.

### Q3 — are the interview_id / scheduled_at filters excluding eligible slots?

Yes, and it's the live bottleneck right now. Snapshotting the current+upcoming window (`thisMonday` 08-03 → `nextMonday` 08-10):

| status | interview_id | scheduled_at | count |
|---|---|---|---|
| drafted | present | ≤24h old or future | 5 |
| drafted | present | >24h old | 13 |
| drafted | present | null | 1 |
| **pending** | **null** | **>24h old (2026-08-05 19:00, ~46h ago)** | **1** |

The sole pending atom is excluded on **both** filters simultaneously: `interview_id is not.null` (permanent — nothing currently backfills an interview onto a planned atom) and `scheduled_at gte. now-24h` (the grace-window floor added when the window was widened on 07-22). Neither filter looks wrong in isolation — an atom with no linked interview genuinely can't be drafted by `draftAtom`, and the 24h floor is a deliberate anti-zombie guard — but together they mean the *entire* eligible pool for pre-draft, right now, is zero. This isn't a caps problem; it's that almost nothing sits in the qualifying state long enough to be counted.

### Q4 — is 60% structurally reachable, or is the denominator wrong?

Two separate structural issues, only one of which is fixed here:

1. **The marker-loss bug (above) is the dominant driver of the gap.** On ground truth, the lane is already at ~64%, above the bar.
2. **The denominator itself has a hard ceiling below 100%, independent of the lane's performance.** Of the 50 `content_items` rows created since 07-22, only 41 trace back to a `content_plan_atoms` row at all (`content_piece_id` join) — the other 9 (18%) came from paths the pre-draft lane structurally cannot reach: `draftOnTopic` ad-hoc requests, video/moment-sourced drafts, and similar. Even if the lane won every single eligible race, the all-drafts share could not exceed ~82%. This doesn't threaten the 60% bar today, but it means "100% predrafted" was never a real target and the review's denominator (all `content_items`) slightly understates lane performance relative to a denominator scoped to atom-sourced drafts only (32/41 = 78% on that narrower, arguably more honest, measure).

### Recommendation for 08-19

- **The live `content_items.voice_audit.predrafted` field can now be trusted directly** — see backfill below; it was previously understating the true rate and needed the ledger as a cross-check, but no longer does.
- **Do not conclude the lane is starved or that the window needs further widening.** The eligible-pool-is-empty finding (Q3) is real but not currently limiting throughput — supply drains fast either via humans or the lane in both regimes observed. If ground-truth share holds ≥60% through 08-19, the correct read is "criterion met, instrumentation was wrong," not "extend the deadline."
- **Backfill shipped 2026-08-07 (same day, post-merge):** ran a scoped, ID-listed `UPDATE` merging `predrafted:true` into `voice_audit` for exactly the 19 clobbered rows identified above (re-verified against fresh prod data immediately before running — same 19, none touched since the fix merged). Verified 19/19 rows updated (`RETURNING id` count matched the prediction), then confirmed ledger vs. live marker agree exactly: **43/43** pre-drafted `content_items` now carry the marker. The 08-19 review can read `content_items.voice_audit.predrafted` directly — no ledger cross-check needed.
